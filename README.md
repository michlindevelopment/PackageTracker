## 🤔 Why AliTrack?

Ever ordered something from AliExpress and then... forgotten about it for two months? 😅

You open the app, scroll for ages trying to find that one earphone case you ordered, copy a tracking number, paste it into a sketchy tracking website that's 80% ads, and find out it's been sitting at customs for a week. **There has to be a better way.**

There is. It's this app. 👇

## ✨ What it does

### 📲 Sign in once, orders import themselves
Sign into AliExpress inside the app and *boom* — your orders show up on their own. Photos, names, prices, tracking numbers, the lot. No copy-pasting, no typing tracking numbers wrong. Ordered something new? One tap on **Refresh** pulls it in.

### 🚚 Real tracking, not guesses
AliTrack talks directly to **Cainiao** — the actual logistics company that moves your AliExpress packages around the world. So instead of "we have no info on this number," you get every scan from "left warehouse in Shenzhen" to "out for delivery near you."

### 🧺 Multi-item orders and combined packages
Ordered three umbrellas in one go? You see **all three pictures** and an item count, not just the first one.

Sellers also love shipping several orders together under one tracking number. AliTrack groups those into a single **combined package**. Tap the card to see the whole shipment — every order with its photos, full name and price, plus a running total. Tap one order inside it to open just that item.

### 💰 Prices and spending
AliTrack reads each order's total from AliExpress (and you can type a price yourself for anything you add by hand). Open **Statistics** from the menu and it turns those prices into answers: *how much did I spend this month, and is that more than last month?* 😬

### 📊 Statistics
- 💸 **Spending** — this month vs last month, total spent, average order, biggest order
- 📈 **Spent per month** — a chart for the last 6 or 12 months
- ⏱️ **Delivery time** — average, fastest, typical and slowest
- 📬 **Delivered per month**
- 🕒 **Still on the way** — how long your most patient order has been waiting
- 🏪 **Top stores** and 🏆 **biggest orders**

### 🔗 Open in AliExpress
One tap from a package's screen takes you to its page on AliExpress — handy for messaging the seller or ordering it again.

### 🔔 Notifications that actually matter
Get pinged when a package's tracking status really changes — shipped, through customs, out for delivery. Not "your package is being processed" 47 times in a row. You can switch it off any time in Settings.

### 🗂️ Two simple lists
- **On the Way** — stuff that's travelling 🛫 (orders the seller hasn't shipped yet live here too, tagged **To Ship** 🕒)
- **Received** — stuff that arrived ✅, with the most recent arrival on top

Find anything fast with **Search**, and reorder the list with **Sort by** (arriving soonest, newest first, oldest first, or A → Z). Both are in the menu.

### 📸 Add a photo
Snap a picture of what you ordered (or use the AliExpress thumbnail) so you can actually remember which package is which. No more "wait, what was this one again?"

### 💬 Delivery texts, optional
Install the optional **SMS add-on** (Settings → SMS matching) and delivery messages from your local courier show up on the right package. It's a separate little app, so AliTrack itself never gets permission to read your messages.

### 🌗 Looks good day or night
Material 3 design with **light, dark, and follow-the-system** themes. On newer phones it even picks up your wallpaper colors. 🎨

### 📅 At-a-glance everything
Estimated delivery, days in transit, a clean timeline of every scan event, where it came from, where it's going. All on one screen.

## 🚀 How to use it

### Adding packages the easy way
1. Tap the **+** button
2. Tap **Sign in to AliExpress** (only once — the app remembers)
3. Sit back — AliTrack pulls in your orders and starts tracking them ✨
4. Later, tap the **Refresh** icon → **Sync packages from AliExpress** to pick up anything new

(Once you're signed in, **+** goes straight to adding a package by hand.)

### Adding a package the manual way
1. Tap the **+** button
2. Type or paste your tracking number
3. Give it a name (optional — but helpful when you have 12 things in transit 😅)
4. Add a price (optional — it feeds the Statistics screen)
5. Snap a photo (also optional, also helpful)
6. Hit **Save** ✅

### Combined packages
- Tap the **card** → the whole shipment, with an **Orders** tab listing everything in it
- Tap an **item inside the card** → just that one item
- In the Orders tab, tap an order to open it, or use **Open in AliExpress**

### Marking something as received
Got your package? 🎁
- **Long-press** the card → **Mark as Received** (it asks you to confirm)
- The same menu is where you **Delete** a package

(Honestly, AliTrack usually figures it out on its own when Cainiao reports it as delivered.)

### If AliExpress signs you out
Sessions expire — it happens. AliTrack tells you with a **Disconnected from AliExpress** message; tap **Connect** and sign in again. You can also use **Refresh → Reconnect to AliExpress** or **Settings → AliExpress**. Your packages stay right where they are; only syncing from AliExpress pauses. 🔌

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is it free?</b></summary>
<br>
Yep. 100%. No ads, no premium tier, no email signup. 🆓
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will it work for non-AliExpress packages?</b></summary>
<br>
AliTrack uses Cainiao's tracking network, which mostly handles AliExpress, Temu, and similar Chinese cross-border shipments. If your package number is from Cainiao, it'll work. Local couriers (DHL, UPS, FedEx, your country's postal service) can't be tracked directly — sorry! 😬 You can still note your local courier's tracking number on a package's **Courier** tab.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why isn't it on the Play Store?</b></summary>
<br>
For now it's distributed directly through GitHub Releases — that keeps it simple and free of Play Store policies. Maybe one day! 🤷
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work in the background?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes! AliTrack quietly checks on your packages roughly once an hour while your phone is online (Android may stretch that to save battery) and pings you when something changes. It can also refresh whenever you open the app — that's the **Sync on app open** switch in Settings. 😴
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why does Statistics say "Not enough order dates yet"?</b></summary>
<br>
Delivery time needs to know when each order was placed, and spending needs its price. AliTrack reads both from your AliExpress order list when it syncs, so the numbers fill in over your next syncs. Older orders only get them if they're part of the sync — to include more of your history, raise the **Completed** budget under **Settings → Import page budgets**. Each card tells you how many orders its numbers are based on. 📊
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What about my privacy?</b></summary>
<br>
Everything stays on your phone. AliTrack only talks to a few places: <b>Cainiao</b> (to ask "where's my package?"), <b>AliExpress</b> (to import your orders and fetch their pictures), and <b>GitHub</b> (to check whether a newer version exists). No analytics, no accounts, no cloud, no ads. Your AliExpress sign-in happens inside the app and is never sent anywhere else. 🔒
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I update the app?</b></summary>
<br>
AliTrack tells you when a new version is out — tap <b>Update</b> in the popup and it downloads and installs right there (Android will ask you to confirm the install). Prefer to do it by hand? Grab the newest APK from the <a href="https://github.com/michlindevelopment/PackageTracker/releases/latest">Releases page</a> and install it on top of the old one. Either way your packages and photos stay put. 🔄
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I disconnect from AliExpress?</b></summary>
<br>
<b>Settings → AliExpress → Disconnect from AliExpress.</b> The packages you've already imported stay; the next sync will ask you to sign in again. 👋
</details>
